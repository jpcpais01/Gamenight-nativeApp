The match

Every touch is physics.

The ball is a real ball. Passes curl, shots dip, keepers parry, and twenty-two players read the game around you. Mobile-style controls make it feel right on a phone, with a keyboard or a gamepad on PC.

  • Passes weighted by how long you hold, through balls timed to the run
  • Sunny afternoons, golden evenings and rainy nights
  • Referees, linesmen, fouls, cards, substitutions and goal replays
  • Walk out of the tunnel to tifos, flares and a full crowd

League mode

Start local. Conquer Europe.

Pick your home country and begin in its local league. Win titles to unlock the regional leagues, then the premiers, then the golden league. Every rival club has its own stadium, colours and goal explosion, so away days feel like away days.

  • A map of Europe with 17 leagues to unlock
  • Fixtures, table, stats and the Sunday paper
  • Play a matchday, simulate it, or watch it play out

Your club

Open packs. Build the squad.

Design your kit, crest, fans and touchline manager. Open card packs to sign new players, from bronze to legends, each with a rating and playstyles like Speedster or Rocket shot. Then pick your formation and your best eleven.

  • Daily free pack, plus Bronze, Silver, Gold, Legend and Ultimate
  • Six formations and a bench of seven
  • Kit designer, crest maker, banners and giant tifos

Stadium builder

Your ground, your rules.

Pick a stand for every side of the pitch from 20 sets, paint each one in 16 colours, then drop the whole thing into one of nine places: a seaside lawn, a city block, an Alpine valley or a white Alentejo hill town. Drag to fly round it, then play your next match there.

  • Arena, Terrace, Curva, The Wall and sixteen more
  • Nine surroundings, from Docklands to a tropical bay
  • Surprise Me builds a matching ground in one tap

Phone as controller

Play the big-screen version on your PC and use your phone as the pad. Scan the QR code on the PC, enter the short pairing code once, and you're in. Works with Android and iPhone over your home Wi-Fi.

Updates in the app

New versions land often. When one is out, a gold Update key appears on the home screen. Tap it and the game downloads and installs the new build for you, on Android and on PC.
